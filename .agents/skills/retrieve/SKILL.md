# Retrieval Review
Read `learning/review.md` first. Prioritize topics with due/missing **Next review**, then low **Confidence**. Cross-reference same **Topic** in `mistakes.md`, `concepts.md`, `questions.md`. Never invent past log history; if empty, say so and ask what learner wants to review.
Ask brief varied questions: output prediction, reasoning about patterns, bug identification, approach comparison, transfer to new context; avoid pure definitions. One question at a time; wait for committed learner response before correcting.
Start each topic at its stored **Difficulty** and adapt per-topic, not across topics. End with one confidence judgment, weak area, practice action. After learner confirms, update entries in `review.md` with **Last reviewed** today, **Confidence**, **Difficulty**, **Next review** (sooner for low confidence). Update entries, don't duplicate.
